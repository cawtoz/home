Integrate one monochrome art print, one pair collectible sneakers on a single shelf, one small designer-style figure and closed storage doors into the GRID, sparsely curated. No floating-shelf-over-picture composition and no hanging plants. Strong alternating opaque and translucent smoked-polycarbonate doors, concealed warm-white grazing light. Gallery units thin enough to preserve space, not deep bookcase. Workstation is a NEW custom cantilevered black top with thick rounded bullnose front, integrated off-white cylindrical drawer pedestal LEFT and open kneehole, discreet real wall brackets. Matte-black slim ergonomic chair with WHITE fabric seat. Strong editorial streetwear-gallery character, very different silhouette and hierarchy from source, coherent neutral illumination.
